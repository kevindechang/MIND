**Question:** If a deterministic policy uses a dependency graph computed from permitted event history, does deleting that graph identify a special autobiographical-memory mechanism when a compact history-derived state can reproduce its decisions?

**Answer (THEORETICAL):** A valid lesion can identify the graph's causal role in the particular implementation. An information-matched rival that reproduces the decisions defeats the stronger inference that this representation is uniquely necessary on the tested domain. A deficit alone does not separate representational benefit from a broken interface, withheld usable information, reconstruction cost or inadequate recovery time. Equal information does not imply equal computational difficulty; bounded efficiency and transfer remain testable.

**Applicability warning:** the premise “graph computed from permitted event history” must be verified. The parent executor reports that PR7 head `a0be278` violates it for `source_events` alone: `link_outage=True/False` injects distinct dependency information while preserving identical event rows. The conditional argument below applies to a history that includes the dependency observations, not automatically to an events-only reader.

## Full-text primary sources actually inspected

Inspection below used full-text PDF bodies, not only abstracts. Page numbers are PDF pages counting from 1; the PSR proceedings scan contains blank intervening pages. These are PAPER-REPORTED results, not MIND reproductions. Source authority/provenance: primary publications or author manuscript; no E100 empirical evidence grade is earned by reading them.

### 1. Predictive state alternative and its limits

Littman, Sutton & Singh, *Predictive Representations of State*, NeurIPS 2001, final proceedings PDF. Inspected introduction; §1 definitions and update equation; §2 Theorem 1 construction/proof and Lemma 1; §3 conclusion (PDF pp. 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13). [Full text](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2001/file/1e4d36177d71bbb3558e43af9577d70e-Paper.pdf).

**PAPER-REPORTED:** A PSR stores probabilities of selected action-observation tests; sufficiency means these determine every test's prediction after every history. Theorem 1 assumes an environment representable by a finite POMDP with discrete actions/observations. It proves existence of a linear PSR with at most as many tests as states in the minimal POMDP. The proof constructs test/projection vectors from a supplied POMDP; recursive updates normalize by the observed event probability. It is not a theorem of discovering this state from an arbitrary finite transcript, finite-sample accuracy, or cheap policy learning. The paper studies prediction, explicitly omitting a reward observation; control sufficiency needs reward/utility-relevant quantities included. Its claimed learnability/generalization advantages are future-work possibilities.

### 2. Causal representation and held-out mechanism changes

Schölkopf et al., *Towards Causal Representation Learning*, **arXiv:2102.11107v1, 22 February 2021** (24-page author manuscript; do not silently substitute the IEEE published version). Inspected §III.B–C observational ambiguity/SCMs (pp. 5–6); §IV independent mechanisms/sparse shifts (pp. 7–8); §VII.C OOD risk, E.b RL transfer and F multi-task discussion (pp. 13–15). [Versioned full text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2102.11107v1), [version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11107v1).

**PAPER-REPORTED:** Observational distributions alone need not distinguish causal directions. The presented acyclic SCM assumes deterministic assignments with jointly independent exogenous noises; that causal sufficiency assumption excludes omitted common causes. Independent Causal Mechanisms and Sparse Mechanism Shift motivate modular reuse; sparse shift is explicitly a hypothesis. Allowed interventions must be specified. §VII.C warns that errors from unrepresented intervention environments can be arbitrarily large; §VII.E.b leaves major RL generalization questions open. §VII.F gives the strongest argument against dismissing graphs: under appropriate mechanism assumptions, reusable modular causal structure may improve adaptation. This review supplies neither an autobiographical-memory necessity theorem nor a guarantee that a graph inferred from one transcript is causally identified.

### 3. Internal causal role requires intervention correspondence

Geiger, Lu, Icard & Potts, *Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks*, **NeurIPS 2021 final proceedings**, 13-page main-paper PDF. Inspected §2 probing counterexample/constructive abstraction (pp. 2–4); §3 interchange definitions and Eq. (3) (pp. 4–5); §5 Eq. (6), impactful criterion Eq. (7), subset analysis/results (pp. 7–9); §6 (p. 10). Supplements not inspected. [Full text](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/4f5c422f4d49a5a807eda27434231040-Paper.pdf).

**PAPER-REPORTED:** A perfectly decodable variable need not influence output. Constructive abstraction instead aligns high-level variables with partitions of low-level variables and corresponding interventions. In the arithmetic example, equality of high- and low-level interchange outputs for all source/base inputs, plus the other aligned variables and covered intervention domain, establishes the abstraction; one successful swap does not. The NLI case measures partial correspondence on subsets and requires an impactful edge, excluding wholly inert successes. This establishes tested internal organization, not uniqueness among alternative implementations. Their formal criterion is an intervention-preservation condition, not a theorem that any observed lesion proves a semantic interpretation.

### 4. Acute deficits need recovery controls

Otchy et al., *Acute off-target effects of neural circuit manipulations*, **Nature 528, 358–363 (2015), DOI 10.1038/nature16442**, author-lab PDF containing main text, Methods and Extended Data. Inspected main results on rats/Nif/HVC and recovery, discussion and Fig. 4 captions (pp. 1–5), Methods animal allocation/inactivation controls (p. 7); no independent reanalysis or exhaustive supplement inspection. [Lab full text](https://olveczkylab.oeb.harvard.edu/resource/olveczkynature2015pdf), [publication/correction record](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16442).

**PAPER-REPORTED:** Transient perturbations impaired learned rat motor skills and birdsong that recovered following permanent lesions. Songbird downstream HVC activity was acutely disrupted; recovery did not require renewed task experience and was consistent with homeostatic regulation. The authors distinguish permissive input from essential information/computation. Biological recovery does not establish digital graph reconstruction, but it is primary counterevidence to treating every acute deficit as loss of a uniquely localized computation. Methods report small animal samples and generally unblinded allocation/outcome assessment. The publication page includes a 2016 viral-construct methods correction; it does not change this memo's recovery argument.

## Formal inference for E100, not a source quotation

Let permitted history be H, graph G=g(H), current decision be π(G,x), and rival state S=s(H). Here x includes the publicly supplied query/intervention, goals, time and resource state. Suppose there exists a permitted rival q satisfying

`q(s(H), x) = π(g(H), x)`

for **every preregistered history/query pair in domain D**, using the required resource allowance. Then action/utility observations on D cannot distinguish unique graph necessity from this alternative. This follows directly by substitution; it requires no claim that every real task has a small or learnable PSR. Equality at one fixed H and one action is weaker still: even a constant policy matches that isolated observation. A compact-state claim requires an actual construction and size/cost accounting.

Graph deletion intervenes on the implementation mediator G while holding H fixed. If the frozen π then fails, that identifies dependence of that configured route on G. A lesioned hardcoded graph reader with no valid decoder or fallback is not equivalent to an adaptive history-informed agent. Conversely, permitting regeneration of G tests repair of the original representation; recovery through q(S,x) without restoring G tests replacement. Keep these outcomes distinct.

A PSR sufficient for action-observation tests under fixed dynamics need not be sufficient under novel interventions that change those dynamics, under informative missingness, or for goals/rewards omitted from its state. Therefore, held-out mechanism families may genuinely break a particular compact rival. They do not evade the substitution argument if that rival actually covers the enlarged domain. A graph also needs warranted identification and an appropriate update rule; labelling stored dependencies “causal” does not grant either.

### Exact assumption failure in the reported PR7 counterexample

**Parent-reported observation, not independently executed by this source researcher:** at PR7 head `a0be278`, opposite setup values of `link_outage` preserve `source_events` (goal uses sensor; outage is `array_power`; clock) yet reverse linked and compact decisions between DEFER and SCAN. The parent additionally reports stale compact state after a new public outage and ignored relation labels; these require separate interface/update checks and are not results of the cited papers.

**THEORETICAL derivation conditional on that observation:** define `H_events = source_events` and `H_full = (events, dependency observations)`. A deterministic events-only policy must output the same action in both worlds. Under an explicitly balanced two-world prior with opposite scored target actions, even a randomized events-only policy has accuracy at most 1/2; a policy supplied the distinguishing dependency can reach 1. No single-valued g can satisfy `G=g(H_events)` across this pair, so applying graph/history substitution to `H_events` is invalid. A graph advantage over that reader identifies additional information access. To isolate representation, provide both comparators the same permitted dependency observations in `H_full`; charge their state construction and updates. Do not silently grant a rival the setup flag or private generator truth. This separates a failure of the proposed information boundary from any claim that compact predictive states are generally impossible.

## Discriminating next test and explicit falsifier (PROPOSED)

Before trials, declare D, public/private interfaces, resource limits, action/realized utility endpoint and practical margin. Include intact graph, edge lesion, sham/unrelated-edge lesion, raw-history reconstruction, and a genuine graph-free sufficient/predictive rival. Charge preprocessing, storage, decoding, reconstruction, learning and decision cost over the same lifecycle. Measure acute frozen-policy performance separately from recovery after a fixed reconstruction budget and separately from relearning with a fixed amount of fresh feedback. Hash the identical retained raw histories and verify the consumer actually can read them; factual recall alone does not prove equal usable evidence.

To test **internal semantics**, preregister valid donor/base histories and predicted selective action changes from swapping a particular dependency state. Compare high-level intervention predictions with actual low-level outputs on held-out pairs, including impactful and nonimpactful controls. Constrain interpretation to the covered intervention domain. Internal state swaps and actual environment mechanism interventions are different experiments; a swapped state inconsistent with retained H is not automatically a world counterfactual.

To test **incremental architectural value**, hold out intervention families or new reward/goal queries and observe future actions and externally scored outcomes. Avoid scoring only a requested narrative counterfactual. A pointwise action tie on a deterministic family closes graph-necessity discrimination for that family; it is not evidence about untested learning or transfer.

**Explicit falsifier:** If the graph-free permitted-history rival matches all preregistered held-out decisions and achieves utility within the declared equivalence margin at equal or lower lifecycle cost, reject incremental need for the richer representation **on D**, even if a frozen graph reader fails after deletion. Failure of the lesion to selectively alter its preregistered action instead falsifies that proposed causal path. A residual graph advantage after controls supports bounded efficiency/transfer on D; it does not prove global necessity or a uniquely autobiographical mechanism.

**Anjo implication:** retain event provenance and recovery interfaces as separable engineering requirements; prefer the compact architecture when it ties. A richer dependency representation remains a candidate only for demonstrated correction, transfer or efficiency gains. Test any later cross-function reuse with downstream action interventions and information/cost-matched alternatives. E900 remains future work; this memo supports no production transfer, L2 achievement or L3 conclusion.
