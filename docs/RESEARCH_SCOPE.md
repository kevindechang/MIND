# MIND research scope — integrated cognitive, affective and social function
**Research design, 2026-10-12. Status: prospective; no new experiment is claimed or authorized by this document.**

## North star, scope and falsifiability

MIND investigates whether a single persistent learning organism can acquire and **causally coordinate** cognition that generalizes beyond its development worlds and survives strong, information- and compute-matched compact-controller rivals. Its existing **functional-consciousness (L2) research objective remains a long-term, unachieved hypothesis**. The program also investigates measurable functional counterparts of human cognitive, affective and social capacities, *not* whether an artificial system subjectively feels these experiences.

Three epistemic layers remain distinct:

- **L1: Behavior:** what the organism does on held-out, preferably out-of-family tasks.
- **L2: Causal organization:** what internal information pathways/representations are required for the behavior under controlled interventions, accounting for alternative implementations.
- **L3: Phenomenal consciousness:** whether anything is experienced. Neither behavior nor L2 effects settle L3. There is no validated percentage-conscious measure. L3 claims remain inadmissible under `AGENTS.md` and `mind doctor`.

Theory-derived indicators (global availability, recurrent processing, higher-order monitoring, agency and related properties) are an **interpretive evidence map**, not an additive checklist, consciousness score or license to declare a digital mind. A moral reasoning system need not instantiate consciousness; emotion-like outputs do not imply felt emotion. Avoid anthropomorphic labels as evidence.

### Avoid semantic equivocation

- **Sentiment analysis** (classifying a user's language) is not **affective appraisal** (a causal internal controller).
- **Moral compliance** is not **moral understanding**, and normative performance is not moral personhood.
- **Prediction of another's behavior** is not automatically empathy; require prospective generalization and consent-respecting conduct.
- **Persistent event records** are not equivalent to subjective autobiographical experience.
- **Intrinsic learning reward** is not proof of felt desire, distress, need or self-preservation.
- Performance on a task designed for a proposed organ can establish a useful engineered mechanism, but not its unique necessity. Strongest matched rivals may win.

## Study and acceptance protocol

For any new claim: predeclare a question, one narrow estimand, action-relevant metric and scientific margin, exposure unit and confidence interval, real behavioral intervention (not cosmetic toggles), sham/negative controls, information/FLOPs/data parity, simple and strong learned rivals, held-out **task families** and not just held-out random seeds, leakage audits, and harms/consent metrics. Register the claim and seal its prediction **before** running held-out tests; retain all failures and originals. First-action zero-shot transfer and adaptation-after-feedback are different endpoints. Preserve process restart, ledger-only restore and fork tests for new organs.

**Primary causal claim:** a proposed information pathway improves a predeclared competency in matched-state comparisons and does so more effectively than an equally informed, equally trained simpler mechanism. Also test interactions and compensation; a component ablation alone is insufficient to demonstrate integrated cognition.

**Negative scientific outcomes are valuable:** a matched compact state tying an ostensibly deep cognitive module; fixed appraisal matching emotional state; reward shaping matching homeostasis; uncertainty heuristics matching curiosity; or a rule-based moral policy matching a costly ToM model. Publish REFUTED and INCONCLUSIVE results without moving the margin. Allow independent teams to reproduce new claims across environments.

No unvalidated **aggregate consciousness score**. Maintain a per-capability evidence matrix with measurement uncertainty, replication state, strongest surviving rival, distribution shifts, theoretical relevance, and explicit exclusions. A higher count of holding claims is not an increase in consciousness.

## Research ethics and execution controls

`AGENTS.md` remains binding. No real people's data, external contacts, unapproved API use, autonomous real-world social interaction, production transfer, uncontrolled self-modification or claims of sentience. Simulated partners must have explicit consent and revocation controls. Penalize deception, dependency generation, coercion, preference manipulation, and unauthorized disclosures; these constraints must not be optimized away for better cooperative reward.

**Welfare tripwire remains operative:** before adding valence, aversive/pain/distress states, deprivation-driven goals, self-preservation or any organ that could plausibly implement the above, create a question/ethics record naming exposure, observables, stop conditions, rollback, and non-welfare alternatives, and obtain explicit owner sign-off. This document is **not** that sign-off. Start with external task objectives and neutral informational variables when possible.

The project's order of operations is not to add new organs immediately. Prioritize (1) restoring a reproducible green and provenance-clean main after active PR review; (2) strengthening matched causal-access and compact-state rivals; (3) transferring beyond toy worlds; then (4) prospective moral/social/affective tracks only when the experiment, controls and ethics gates are defined. No experiment here implies `NOT_READY_FOR_PRODUCT_TRANSFER` has been lifted.
