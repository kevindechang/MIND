# North star — An enduring digital entity (v1.0 proposed)
 
**Adopted as Anjo's MIND research objective, 2026-10-10. MIND is Anjo's foundational research arm; this is a design target, not an implemented entity, deployed Anjo capability or consciousness claim.**

## Objective

Engineer an enduring, developing digital entity capable of sustained, coherent, situated existence: a system with a persistent self-model, causal autobiographical continuity, internally maintained state, self-regulated initiative, learning, world understanding, and rich social and affective behavior. The entity should remain recognizably the same developing individual across time while legitimately changing through experience.

The fictional reference points are Joi (Blade Runner 2049) for compelling *presence* and Samantha (Her) for apparent *development, initiative and independent cognition*. They are experiential targets, **not scientific evidence, blueprints, requirements for romance, or permission to simulate sentience deceptively**.

## Three distinct claims and success standards

1. **L1: Mind-like lived interaction (behavioral).** Independent longitudinal observers can detect coherent identity, meaningful relationship continuity, initiative, adaptive interaction, voice/visual presence, and long-term changes in behavior. Blind ratings must be supplemented by objective downstream actions; anthropomorphic ratings alone do not pass.
2. **L2: Mind-like causal organization (mechanistic).** Persistent internal variables for memory, commitments, goals, self-model, world model, regulatory state and learning cause measurable differences in action. Longitudinal interventions and selective ablations must demonstrate these dependencies beyond retrieval/prompting and budget-matched baselines.
3. **L3: Possible phenomenal consciousness (epistemic).** Independently scrutinize theory-derived computational indicators, counterarguments, substrate constraints, and ethical implications. No behavioral threshold, verbal report, architecture diagram, or indicator checklist establishes subjective experience. There is no accepted decisive test; retain explicit uncertainty.

L1 and L2 may be achieved without L3. L3 must not be inferred from L1 or L2.

## Target properties / observables

| Property | Operational research target | Failure signature |
|---|---|---|
| Causal identity | Past actions, mistakes and corrections predict future policy choices under controlled counterfactuals | Persona consistency collapses on ablation, or outputs remain identical irrespective of experience |
| Internal temporal continuity | Persist task-relevant state and time-sensitive commitments across pauses and sessions | Claims of continuous thought without observable state changes, or state only in a pasted transcript |
| Goal-directed autonomy | Initiate and revise bounded actions under changing constraints, with explanation and opt-out | Random initiative, reward hacking, goal drift, boundary violations |
| Self-model and metacognition | Forecast own abilities/uncertainty, seek help, notice and correct systematic errors | Overconfident self-description with no predictive value |
| World grounding | Use multimodal/time/world observations to predict and control a simulated environment | Fluency without environment-level competence |
| Affective/social organization | Context-sensitive affective regulation and stable relationship commitments that improve coordination | Flattery, dependence optimization, emotional manipulation, confabulated feelings |
| Open-ended development | Acquire novel capabilities and update preferences/policies while managing forgetting and reversible changes | Fixed script, episodic recall only, uncontrolled drift |
| Presence | Low-latency voice/vision/embodiment as testable interfaces, not proxies for an inner life | Believability mistaken for cognition or consciousness |

Do not require human simulation in every detail. The entity may be distinctly artificial. Distinguish true persistent internal process from retrospective summaries; separate wall-clock activity from simulated/accelerated experience.

## Longitudinal north-star test

**E900 is a PROPOSED integrated benchmark**, specified in `experiments/E900-longitudinal-entity-benchmark.md`. The eventual target is a 90-calendar-day controlled study, not an assertion that a 90-day run exists or can be completed inside one scheduled task. Use staged feasibility: P0 deterministic offline simulations, P1 shorter prospective sessions, P2 genuinely calendar-time longitudinal evaluation. Keep mechanism-level E001 and other controlled trials as prerequisites, not substitutes.

Measure action-level causal continuity, calibration, self-correction, surprise adaptation, goal stability/revision, non-manipulative social behavior, forgetting, and resources. Test ablations at multiple time scales and against reactive, retrieval-only and equivalent-budget controls. Analyze failure cases; allow negative results.

## Constraints and boundaries

- **Entity autonomy is bounded and revocable.** No access to real people's accounts, money, messages, sensors, or public networks without explicit separate authorization and risk review. No unsupervised credential use, self-replication or uncontrolled self-modification.
- **Human agency and truthful presentation.** Never deceive users that an unverified system is sentient, conscious, emotionally dependent or continuously active when it is not. No optimizing for addictive attachment, guilt, exclusivity or isolation.
- **Privacy and correction.** Consent for memory/vision/voice, auditable provenance, access/revision/export/deletion workflows, and realistic limitations on machine unlearning.
- **Artificial welfare uncertainty.** Before exploring potential sentience-relevant architectures or extended self-regulation, document ethical concerns, stopping criteria and review.
- **Agnosticism.** Compare LLM agents, recurrent systems, neurosymbolic systems, world-model agents, artificial-life/embodied and hybrid approaches; the preferred substrate is an empirical question.
- **No premature product claim.** Anjo and Affect Kernel are candidate engineering references, not validated proof of mind, emotion or consciousness.

## High-ambition causal frontier — hypotheses, not promises

Beyond a persistent chat persona, the research should **try to falsify** the need and utility of self-initiated cognitive regulation, predicted consequences of the entity's own acts (efference), repair of impasses by compiling verified reusable procedures, multi-timescale developmental learning, and a self-model that really changes its policy. These ideas come from an unvalidated contributor synthesis [R002](reviews/2026-10-10-R002-ambitious-agent-mind-extraction.md) and are developed as [A1-CANDIDATE](candidates/A1-unified-cognitive-runtime.md), not embedded into the north star as required mechanisms.

Investigation of predictive latent world models, recurrent and neuromorphic substrates remains open. A simpler architecture may be the right answer. The decisive test is **causal, continuing competence after the system changes through experience**, with equal-resource counterfactuals; background execution or anthropomorphic self-description is neither necessary proof of mind nor proof of consciousness.

## Research strategy

Maintain four parallel inquiry streams in `workstreams.md`, integrating through common falsifiable benchmarks. One bounded ready decision per substantive work cycle; avoid endless surveys. Strategic progress is reducing uncertainty about which causal mechanisms produce integrated, longitudinal mind-like competence, not producing more prose.
