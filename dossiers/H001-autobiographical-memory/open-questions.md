## Near-term G0 → G2 boundary

G0 requires an operational metric, environment population, comparable arms, leakage controls and observable oracle. G1 requires genuinely competing predictions. G2 requires a **frozen** protocol specifying quantitative effect target, seed/power/precision, cost matching, stopping and exclusion rules. None of these latter decisions can be retroactively inferred from simulator correctness.

## Update protocol

When resolving one open question, record a date, exact prior hypothesis ID, method or source inspected, decision status, what would refute it, experiment link and new dependency. Never erase falsified questions or retroactively claim they were settled. [Canonical state](../../research-state.md) governs the live next action.
