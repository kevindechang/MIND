## Allocation and cadence

One ready decision per invocation under [research-cycle](../research-cycle.md). [Work accounting](../research-records.md) separates attention from accepted contributions. Reserve a non-leading-stream opportunity in every four new completed substantive work records with justified exceptions. Synthesize every eight new distinct valid accepted contributions or sooner after major validated evidence/pivotal invalidation; historical 008 is not an automatic milestone. Governance and SKIPs count toward neither.

After three substantive unchanged attempts, reassess before another; prioritize decision impact, uncertainty reduction, feasibility and integration. Missing model execution is a named block, not authorization for paid infrastructure.

## Mechanism admission

A proposed A0/A1 module is not adopted because it is biologically inspired, fashionable, easy to diagram or narratively compelling. Require a distinct predicted downstream effect, preregistered ablation, equal-resource competitors, negative controls, replicable benefit or justified cost/robustness tradeoff, and ethical acceptability. Negative results can terminate expensive directions.
