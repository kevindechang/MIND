## Abstract
Answer one sharp scientific question with an honest uncertainty statement. Separate known research and new local observations.

## 1. Constructs, history and philosophical stakes
Operationalize terms. Give the disciplinary differences rather than claiming consensus.

## 2. Theories and competing mechanisms
Minimum: strongest accepted/novel mechanism, simpler rival, non-obvious disconfirming mechanism. State mutually distinguishing predictions and assumptions.

## 3. Evidence map and search reproducibility
Table: source ID/link | date/type | experiment/sample | method actually read (abstract/full/methods) | support/contradiction | limitations | evidence status.

## 4. Formalization and identification
Define state, inputs, actions, outputs, causal graph if relevant, estimands and needed assumptions. Derive where a competitor's sufficient state makes a richer mechanism unnecessary. Clarify unknowable cases.

## 5. Experimental/engineering audit
Link the real preregistration, code, logs, failure cases and actual completed results; do not substitute intentions for observations.

## 6. Critical interdisciplinary synthesis
What survived, what failed, why evidence cannot be pooled, which human-neuroscience or philosophical transfer assumptions are unjustified.

## 7. Architecture/E900 implications
Which named module hypothesis is supported, weakened or untouched? Separate L1/L2/L3, costs, privacy and sentience uncertainty.

## 8. Open questions and exact next test
Rank questions by decision value, required evidence and falsifier. One executable next step, no manufactured certainty.

## 9. Revision ledger
Date/version → changed claim/source/experiment → evidence/uncertainty change → review mode.

## Compounding transfer fields

- Stable decision ID/claim anchor; pivotal evidence version and strongest rival.
- Outcome → action mapping, scoped support and uncertainty; null is not equivalence.
- Prerequisite/dependent decision IDs, freshness and transitive reassessment if evidence fails.
- Reused asset ID/version, assumptions, verification provenance and revalidation.
- Proposed E900 observable/cross-function intervention; component win is not integration or L3.
- Work kind, scientific verdict and distinct contribution ID; amendments do not multiply acceptance.
- Attention eligibility vs accepted-science eligibility under [work accounting](../research-records.md).
- Exactly one next executable action, required tools/inputs/deliverable/stop condition.
