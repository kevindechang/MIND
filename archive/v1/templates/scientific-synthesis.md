## Architecture tournament
For each architecture candidate: claim, competitor, cost and evidence parity, disconfirming result, adopted/rejected/unchanged status. Include an intentionally simple option.

## Contradictions, replication and unknowns
State conflicts and their explanatory alternatives. Distinguish independent replication from simulator unit tests or multiple prompts.

## Integrated-entity implications
Is any E900 P0/P1/P2 claim actually supported by same-agent longitudinal intervention? Separate L1 behavior, L2 causal mechanisms, L3 philosophical uncertainty.

## Decisions and handoff
Accept/revise/reject/defer with evidence links, versioned architecture updates if justified, top decision-changing next experiment, risk/ethics and research budget.

## Publication/version history
Dated signed or attributable review record, exact source IDs and GitHub SHA. No invented independent reviewer.

## Compounding transfer fields

- Stable decision ID/claim anchor; pivotal evidence version and strongest rival.
- Outcome → action mapping, scoped support and uncertainty; null is not equivalence.
- Prerequisite/dependent decision IDs, freshness and transitive reassessment if evidence fails.
- Reused asset ID/version, assumptions, verification provenance and revalidation.
- Proposed E900 observable/cross-function intervention; component win is not integration or L3.
- Work kind, scientific verdict and distinct contribution ID; amendments do not multiply acceptance.
- Attention eligibility vs accepted-science eligibility under [work accounting](../research-records.md).
- Exactly one next executable action, required tools/inputs/deliverable/stop condition.
