# Research Atlas — template (copy into atlas/ as needed)

**Version, date, editors, status:** PROPOSED; this file is a template, not an active finding.

## Grand question and minimal definitions
State the target without anthropomorphic shortcuts; distinguish L1/L2/L3 and observable constructs. Specify what is deliberately out of scope.

## Scientific priorities
Table: priority ID | hypothesis | scientific importance | strongest competing approach | what would change our mind | dependencies | current evidence grade.

## DARPA Heilmeier review
Answer the eight questions: objective in plain language, current methods, novelty, who cares, risks, financial/resource ceiling, realistic/uncertain horizon, and intermediate/final tests.

## Science Traceability Matrix
Table: goal ID | objective | measurement and observable | implementation/instrument | experimental intervention | failure condition | evidence artifact and grade.

## Integration strategy
Show theory → experiment → architecture and ethical gates; clarify when components aren't valid evidence of whole-system cognition.

## Decision and version history
List strategic decisions with dated counterarguments, sources and rejections. Preserve prior versions and reviewer provenance.

## Decision and reuse trace

Link objectives to [decisions](../decision-register.md), strongest rivals, pivotal evidence/freshness, [assets](../asset-register.md) and E900 causal coupling interventions. Use [work accounting](../research-records.md) for attention and accepted-science synthesis milestones. A diagram or inventory does not establish functional consciousness or phenomenal experience.
