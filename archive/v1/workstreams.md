# Research workstreams and integration contract (v1.0 proposed)

Research is parallel in *scope*, but one scheduled invocation works on **one** well-defined question. A scheduled task is not an always-running lab or proof of a live entity.

## W1 — Functional digital entity

**Question:** Which measurable structures create robust identity, internal temporal continuity, stable agency, learning and social behavior in one developing agent?

Initial queue:
- B001 / E001: causal autobiographical memory vs no memory, retrieval, sham and edge ablation.
- W1-02: measurable self-model, correction and hidden capability-change tests (H002).
- W1-03: autonomy and internal state during non-interaction, distinguishing real persisted state updates from narratives about them; E002a proposes direct comparison to dormant, fixed schedule and simple priority queue (H005/H008).
- W1-04: social/affective integrity tests including respectful disagreement, calibration and relationship boundaries.

## W2 — Architecture, learning and substrates

**Question:** Which computational mechanisms are necessary or efficient, rather than decorative?

Initial queue:
- Direct orchestration vs global workspace broadcast (H003).
- Recurrent state vs longer-context or more test-time compute (H004).
- Stable plasticity: external state, online learned rules, adapters and weight changes at matched compute/retention budgets (H012); no zero-forgetting guarantee.
- R002's ambitious mechanisms: causal efferent copies/self–world attribution (H010/E002b), impasse-driven verified reusable skills (H011/E003), true endogenous cognitive maintenance (H005/H008/E002a), and causal self-model integration (H002/H007).
- Predictive processing, active inference, artificial-life, embodied, neurosymbolic, recurrent and other non-LLM candidates; compare optional hierarchical latent world models / JEPA-family and event-driven/neuromorphic substrates without assuming superiority.
- Voice, vision and embodiment only when a mechanism or interface outcome is testable.

## W3 — Consciousness science

**Question:** What would competing theories predict for artificial systems, and what evidence would genuinely discriminate them?

Cover global workspace, recurrent processing, higher-order, IIT, attention schema, predictive processing/active inference, and substrate-dependent objections. Track (a) theory commitments, (b) implemented mechanisms, (c) accessible evidence, (d) contradictory results, and (e) transfer limits from humans/animals to software. Never convert self-reported experience or a behavior rating into proof.

## W4 — Philosophy, identity, governance and welfare

**Question:** What constitutes a persisting artificial individual, what cannot currently be verified about experience, and how should autonomous social entities be designed responsibly?

Compare psychological continuity, functionalism, biological naturalism, computationalism, enactivism and competing objections. Track consent, truthful self-presentation, relational manipulation, privacy, interruptibility and uncertain artificial welfare. Philosophy can change definitions and protocols; it cannot serve as empirical validation.

## Architecture candidate governance

R002's [A1 Unified Cognitive Runtime](candidates/A1-unified-cognitive-runtime.md) is a **SPECULATIVE competitor**, not the selected architecture. Its five ambitious mechanisms are extracted in [R002 intake](reviews/2026-10-10-R002-ambitious-agent-mind-extraction.md). Any claimed advantage must survive strong simple baseline comparisons and causal ablation; do not mistake trace instrumentation, always-on computing or human-like conversation for consciousness.

## Integration: system-level entity evaluation

- **E900** (PROPOSED) integrates W1–W4, never replaces their mechanism tests.
- Require evidence that a single persistent agent's mechanisms interact over time; module-specific successes cannot be added up into a mind.
- Every architectural claim links to an ablation, control or source-labeled speculation; comparisons include strong minimal systems.
- Maintain a versioned claim ladder L1 behavior / L2 causal organization / L3 unresolved consciousness.

## Allocation and anti-stagnation

Choose one ready decision per cycle by expected decision impact, uncertainty reduction, integration relevance, cost and feasibility. E001/D001 is first experimental priority; unrelated ready decisions may proceed with an explicit contribution to integration. Maintain prerequisite/dependent links in [decision-register](decision-register.md).

Use [research-records](research-records.md) for accounting: at least one non-leading-stream opportunity in every four new completed substantive work records, unless safety or execution-critical dependencies justify an exception. Governance, skipped invocations and superficial repetition do not count. Attention records and accepted science are distinct; historical W2/004 records attention but remains MAJOR_REVISION_REQUIRED and does not initialize prospective accepted counts.

After three substantive attempts without gate movement, resolved uncertainty or changed decision, reassess before another. Simplify, retire an invalid benchmark, resolve a capability or move to a ready dependency; continued focus needs new expected information gain. No source-count, title or prose reset.

Synthesize after eight new distinct valid accepted contributions, or earlier for major validated evidence/pivotal invalidation, with milestone IDs. Do not infer this from legacy 008 or from stored instruments. Preserve W3/W4 relevance and E900 coupling tests without treating philosophy as empirical validation or a module win as an integrated mind.
