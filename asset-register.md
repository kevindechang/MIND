## Reuse contract

Each cycle records asset ID/version, assumed input/environment constraints, whether assumptions still hold, modifications and required revalidation. Keep old versions accessible. A locally checked instrument may be reused for calibration without promoting a mechanism. A new family, oracle or observation restriction requires revalidation; an old passing test does not validate changed semantics.

Entries may also be full-method source extractions, negative findings or formal arguments. Record exact source depth, inputs/assumptions, derivation/results, counterexample and limitations. No compulsory code artifact or source-count quota. State dependencies explicitly and flag stale entries when their pivotal premises fail.
