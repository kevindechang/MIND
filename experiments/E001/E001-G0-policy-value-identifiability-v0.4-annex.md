## Cognitive-method counterexample and expanded intervention proposal

Full accepted-manuscript methods/results inspected: Bramley, Dayan, Griffiths & Lagnado (2017), *Formalizing Neurath's Ship: Approximate Algorithms for Online Causal Learning*, *Psychological Review* 124:301–338, DOI 10.1037/rev0000061, [UCL full accepted manuscript](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/1518681/1/Bramley_neuraths_ship.pdf). Their causal systems are probabilistic noisy-OR, with 3–4 binary variables; participants may fix components ON, OFF or leave them free, which is a strictly **richer intervention family** than E001's single-node reachability probes. Experiment 1 had 120 Mechanical Turk participants, four strength/noise conditions; Experiment 2 had 111 undergraduates, unknown parameters and alternate display/elicitation conditions. Across three datasets, model fitting compared Neurath's-ship local single-hypothesis updates, simple endorsement, win-stay/lose-sample, random edits and a rational Bayesian model, with BIC and model-recovery analyses. Authors report aggregate preference for local approximation (overall NS BIC 93381 vs SE 94326; individual fits heterogeneous), while model recovery is imperfect and simple endorsement wins one experiment. These are published **human task behavior fits**, not evidence of artificial mind or a proof graph memory is better than compact belief. Our exact eight-world belief policy is an ideal reference, not a psychologically realistic algorithm under unbounded graph spaces.

**Constructive falsifier of our limited observation channel:** introduce a new *path-blocking* probe that activates node0 while clamping node1 so it cannot transmit influence. For chain graph5 (0→1→2), the observable reach mask is 3; for graph7 with direct 0→2, mask is 7. Local independent deterministic check **PASS**. This does not refute the v0.4 seven-class theorem, because it changes the permitted probe alphabet. It demonstrates that the benchmark's non-identification is **instrument-relative**, and suggests a genuinely discriminating Stage-1 extension. Before any such extension, preregister the physical/causal semantics of clamps, cost, timing, censor likelihood, comparator access and graph family. A richer probe is not automatically a more mind-like mechanism.
