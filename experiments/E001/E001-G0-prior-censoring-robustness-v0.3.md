## Formal model, assumptions and executable boundary

Candidate graph G lies in the v0.2 forward-DAG family (8 structures on {0,1,2}) or a specified two-graph subset. Every issued probe j carries public event ID, node a_j, issue time, due time, and the decision deadline. For due events, public status is DELIVERED with a mask y_j, or CENSORED; future-due events remain PENDING and convey no evidence **only when due times themselves are fixed publicly and do not depend on G**. Each public model supplies prior p(G) and a delivery/censor likelihood c(G,a), the probability of censoring a due event. Censoring indicators are conditionally independent given G and issued probes for this narrow validation; delivered mask is noiseless reachability r_G(a). Probe selection may be adaptive only to earlier public history (nonanticipating).

Up to a normalizing constant, the correct conditional posterior is

`p(G|h) proportional to p(G) * product_due_censored c(G,a_j) * product_due_delivered [(1-c(G,a_j))*1{y_j=r_G(a_j)}]`.

The likelihood of a public-history-conditioned adaptive action cancels from Bayesian model comparison when selection is nonanticipating and the *actual public action history is conditioned upon*. This cancellation does NOT extend to hidden-informed probe policies, graph-dependent delivery delays, hidden confounding or an unknown mechanism. Pre-deadline pending events are not assimilated as negative feedback. If a probe is due before the deadline yet lacks both delivered/censored status, the stricter v0.3 wrapper fails closed rather than silently assuming missing at random. Invalid zero likelihood fails closed.

The executable extension [e001_three_node_robustness.py](../instruments/e001_three_node_robustness.py) imports the existing v0.2 public-history validation, reachability and utility semantics; it adds: arbitrary exact rational prior weights; graph-indexed public censor probabilities; `conditional_posterior`, `conditional_score`, and explicitly separate `robust_score`. The robust score is `max_a min_{k in public ambiguity bank} E_{G~p_k(G|h)}[U(a,G)]`; it is not a posterior average over the bank, and it is not an optimal probe-acquisition policy. Only histories, public prior/censor-model and predeclared candidate family enter scoring. A graph-indexed likelihood is a public hypothesis about possible worlds, *not* a reference to the evaluator's sampled private G.

## Exact adversarial results

1. **Informative missingness / failure of v0.2's uniform inference.** Take G0 empty and G1 full forward-DAG; p(G0)=p(G1)=1/2; probe node 2, whose delivered reachability mask is `100` in *both* worlds. Announce censor probabilities `c(G0,2)=1/4`, `c(G1,2)=3/4`. For the identical public CENSORED history, posterior becomes `(1/4,3/4)`; when the common `100` mask is delivered, `(3/4,1/4)`. For graph-independent c=1/2, censoring leaves `(1/2,1/2)`. Thus **flagging a censored event is necessary but not sufficient to make missingness ignorable**. v0.2's declared 8-graph posterior conditions solely on delivered effects, so this is a valid limitation of that conditional scoring interpretation, not a new hidden-graph leakage bug.
2. **Prior-dependent best actions.** With no probes, two worlds as above, commitments (goal0=1, penalty8) and (goal2=1, penalty8), 7 plans of length <=2 and utility `-(loss+len(plan))`: a prior (9,1) over (empty,full) selects plan `(0,2)` (Bayes utility -14/5); the reverse prior (1,9) selects `(0,)` (-9/5). Under finite ambiguity bank {(9,1),(1,9)}, maximin selects `(0,)` at -41/5 worst expectation. Prior choice changes the label without changing *any agent-permitted history*. Comparisons must declare the score's public model, not the sampled graph.
3. **Misspecification.** A reverse edge 2->0 is outside the eight *forward* DAGs. Probe node 2 produces mask `101`, impossible in every declared candidate, and throws a zero-normalizer error. But no probe or a nondiscriminating history can conceal that mismatch; the instrument has **detection conditional on observations**, not universal structural identification.
4. **Deadline and event integrity.** A pending event due after T does not alter the posterior under fixed delays. A due event with neither outcome nor censor marker is rejected; a censor flag before the due time is rejected. Existing v0.2 event-ID pairing and delivered-after-deadline guards are reused. This still does **not** implement an enforced maximum probe count, probe cost, policy-level expected value, stochastic effect noise, or a real reset.

The falsifier `same public h + same prior/censor model + same declared family -> distinct score` was not observed. Equal-history equality must NOT be asserted across different public likelihood assumptions. Claim label: OBSERVED local deterministic instrument behavior; the sufficiency argument below is THEORETICAL conditional on the stated model.
