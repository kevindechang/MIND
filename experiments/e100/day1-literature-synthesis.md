# E100 Day 1 — Broadcast lesion literature synthesis v0.1 (PROPOSED, not accepted science)

Review: SELF-REVIEW_ONLY. Inspection depth recorded per source; nothing below was replicated.
Question: what must a Day 1 two-consumer broadcast lesion control to be discriminating (H003)?
Decision impact: shapes Day 1 preregistration (delivery-only cut, matched SELECT parity, wipe/alternate/completion controls, point-to-point + compact rivals, null on tie). No architecture selected.

## Source-level method extraction

### 1. Global Neuronal Workspace as broadcast (theory)

- **Dehaene, Kerszberg & Changeux 1998; Dehaene & Naccache 2001; Dehaene 2014** — PAPER-REPORTED via secondary (Wajnerman Paz 2024; Naccache et al. 2025; Cogitate 2025). Depth: NOT directly inspected; do not cite as verified. Claim: specialized parallel processors; attentional amplification triggers brain-scale ignition; long-range workspace neurons make selected content globally available to categorization, memory, evaluation, action. Transfer caveat: functionalist framework; ignition/synchrony signatures are neural, not implementable as ledger reads. Contrary: see Cogitate challenges below.
- **Wajnerman Paz 2024, "The global neuronal workspace as a broadcasting network" (PMC)** — Depth: abstract + excerpts. Claim: broadcasting is GNW's main function; proposes graph-theoretic small-world characterization (reduced path length, reduced modularity during conscious access). Design: theoretical synthesis, no new experiment. Limitation: no causal test of broadcast vs alternatives. Transfer: motivates Day 1's "cut delivery, preserve record" shape only.
- **Baars 1988/1993 (founder)** — PAPER-REPORTED via Goyal et al. 2021, VanRullen & Kanai 2021, Dossa et al. 2024. Depth: not inspected. Used only for provenance.

### 2. Adversarial GNW vs IIT (strongest empirical caution)

- **Cogitate Consortium et al. 2025, Nature, "Adversarial testing of GNW and IIT" (n=256, fMRI+MEG+iEEG, preregistered)** — Depth: Nature page + ADS/PubMed excerpts inspected; full PDF/analyses NOT replicated. Design: suprathreshold stimuli, variable durations; P1 content decoding (posterior vs PFC), P2 maintenance (sustained posterior vs onset/offset PFC ignition 0.3–0.5s + silent maintenance), P3 interareal synchrony. Reported: content decodable in visual/ventrotemporal/inferior-frontal; sustained occipital/lateral-temporal duration tracking; content-specific fronto-visual sync — but general lack of offset ignition and limited PFC representation of orientation/category (challenges GNW), and lack of sustained posterior synchrony (challenges IIT). Baseline: theory-neutral consortium, Bayesian ROI tests (BF01>3 reported for PFC nulls; 62–94% voxels evidence for no interareal connectivity in some ROIs). Limitation (self-reported + rebuttal): only clearly visible stimuli — the conscious vs non-conscious contrast central to GNWT untested. Transfer caveat: human neural outcome; says NOTHING about whether a ledger broadcast is good engineering.
- **Naccache, Sergent, Dehaene, Wang, Farisco & Changeux 2025, Neurosci. Consciousness (GNW rebuttal)** — Depth: excerpts inspected. Counter: onset ignition 200–800ms irrespective of duration/relevance + fronto-visual sync VALIDATE non-trivial GNW predictions; offset ignition is NOT a central prediction unless offset awareness enforced; PFC decoding is hard due to mixed selectivity (vs columnar visual cortex); posterior decoding success may include non-conscious processes. Lesson for Day 1: predeclare which contrast is central BEFORE running; do not redescribe a null as support after the fact. Our preregistration follows this by freezing P1–P7.

### 3. AI broadcast implementations (direct design precedents)

- **Goyal et al. 2021 (ICLR 2022), "Coordination Among Neural Modules Through a Shared Global Workspace"** — Depth: arXiv abstract + section excerpts. Design: specialists compete (key-query-value attention) for limited-capacity write access; workspace contents broadcast to all specialists (second attention step); tested on RIMs/Transformers/TIMs/Universal Transformers. Reported: capacity limits encourage specialization/compositionality and synchronization. Baselines: pairwise self-attention communication (no bottleneck). Limitation: workspace not recurrent (fails GWT-4 per Dossa et al.); no lesion-ablation of broadcast edge reported in inspected excerpts. Transfer: Day 1 copies the two-step shape (compete/write → broadcast) at ledger scale; bottleneck size effect (Dossa) warns a minimal task may show rival tie.
- **VanRullen & Kanai 2021, "Deep Learning and the Global Workspace Theory"** — Depth: abstract + excerpts. Roadmap: unsupervised neural translation between latent spaces → Global Latent Workspace; broadcast = automatic translation into all modules' latent spaces (availability ≠ use); penumbra concept. No experiment. Use: defines broadcast as availability, which Day 1 implements as "SELECT executed, rows discarded on lesion."
- **Dossa, Arulkumaran, Juliani, Sasai & Kanai 2024, Front. Comput. Neurosci., embodied GWA in SoundSpaces/SAVi audiovisual navigation** — Depth: full-text §§1–2.7 inspected via publisher fetch (results §§3–4 partially truncated, NOT relied on). Design (key precedent): GWA satisfies RPT-1/2 + GWT-1–4; broadcast implemented as `wm_{t-1}` fed back into each modality GRU encoder; BASELINE ABLATES BROADCAST by preventing `wm_{t-1}` → encoders (retains recurrence, GWT-1; removes GWT-2/3/4 attention). Training: behavioral cloning on 500k oracle tuples (not RL, to equalize observation-action distribution). Reported (from inspected abstract/discussion excerpts): GWA better/more robust at SMALL working-memory sizes; difference disappears as bottleneck grows; smaller workspaces develop mixed cross-modal attention; encoders prefer broadcast over direct recurrent feedback. Limitation: imitation, not online RL; single task family; benefit conditional on bottleneck size. Transfer: Day 1's lesion (sever delivery, preserve store) and matched-distribution logic are directly modeled on this ablation; the bottleneck-conditional benefit motivates our predeclared rival-tie rule.
- **Juliani et al. 2022b (Perceiver+GWT)** — PAPER-REPORTED via Dossa et al. Depth: not inspected. Noted: Perceiver lacks broadcast to preprocessing modules (fails GWT-2/3) until refined. Caution only.

### 4. Lesion/ablation validity (strongest methodological rival)

- **Fakhar & Hilgetag 2022, PLoS Comput. Biol., "Systematic perturbation of an ANN"** — Depth: PubMed/PLOS/abstract excerpts; full lesion matrices NOT replicated. Design: exhaustive lesions of a small evolved arcade ANN; single-element SPA vs Multi-perturbation Shapley (MSA) over all multi-site combinations. Reported: single-site lesions BIASED; multi-site MSA captured missed coalitions; diaschisis (network-wide effects without direct connections); second ablations can reverse first-ablation disruption. Limitation for Day 1: our single-edge lesion in a zero-redundancy ledger works BY CONSTRUCTION and cannot claim general lesion-validity; multi-path systems need MSA-style analysis (deferred to Days 2–7).
- **Belrose et al. 2024 (NeurIPS), "Optimal ablation"** — Depth: excerpts. Method: replace ablated edge with learned constant to separate "deleted information" from "introduced noise"; OA prediction maps beat prior tracing on causal faithfulness. Lesson: Day 1's lesioned reader returns a fixed `NO_ACTION` (constant), consistent with OA discipline, and still executes the SELECT to avoid noise confounds.
- **Geiger, Lu, Icard & Potts 2021, "Causal Abstractions of Neural Networks"** — Depth: excerpts. Method: align representations to high-level causal variables; verify with interchange interventions; probing alone is non-causal. Lesson: Day 1's `based_on` provenance + wipe/alternate interchange checks follow this pattern at toy scale.
- **Li, Davies & Nadeau 2024 ("Circuit Breaking") / Conmy et al. 2023 / Geiger et al. 2022** — Depth: excerpts. Method: targeted edge ablation removing bad-behavior circuits (12/11.6k edges for GPT-2 toxicity) with minimal collateral damage. Lesson: edge-level cuts are feasible instruments; collateral (primary-consumer) stability must be reported — Day 1 P2 does this.

### 5. Communication-causality and matched-budget rivals

- **Lowe et al. 2019 (AAMAS), "On the Pitfalls of Measuring Emergent Communication"** — Depth: arXiv abstract + excerpts. Design: matrix games + communication channel; metrics: reward gain, signalling correlation (message↔action), CIC causal influence of communication (positive listening). Reported: agents can APPEAR to communicate (messages informative about speaker's next action) while messages have ZERO effect on listener/environment (ablation + policy visualization). Lesson: Day 1 REQUIRES downstream-action change under lesion (CIC-style positive listening), not intact correlation; wipe/alternate controls implement this.
- **Eccles et al. / Jaques et al. (biases for emergent communication); Lazaridou & Baroni 2020; Cao et al. 2018** — Depth: excerpts via survey. Note: RIAL/DIAL need inductive biases; centralized training limits decentral claims. Lesson: do not mistake channel presence for learned communication.
- **Ann, Liu & Tan 2026 (ICML), "The Interaction Tax"** — Depth: abstract excerpt. Design: 11 verifier-scored optimization tasks, matched budgets; full-solution sharing vs independent proposals. Reported: full interaction erases diversity within one round; independent sampling captures most gains; critique helps only for easy-to-find violations. Lesson (strongest Day 1 rival logic): under matched budgets, SHARING can be neutral/negative; compact/independent rivals must be run and may win. Day 1 predeclares tie→retain-simpler.

## Cross-cutting synthesis for Day 1

1. Broadcast has converging theoretical motivation (GWT) and AI precedents (shared workspace, GLW, embodied GWA) but CONDITIONAL empirical support: bottleneck-size-dependent (Dossa), adversarially challenged in humans (Cogitate), and causally fragile under naive metrics (Lowe) and naive lesions (Fakhar).
2. A discriminating Day 1 must therefore: (a) cut delivery not storage; (b) hold SELECT budgets equal; (c) show downstream change + source stability; (d) pass wipe/alternate/completion interchanges; (e) run point-to-point AND compact-state rivals with ≤ resources and retire broadcast on tie.
3. In the minimal finite-state ledger these conditions are satisfiable by construction — which validates the INSTRUMENT, not H003. Any L2 claim needs Days 2–7 interference/held-out/counterfactual designs with multi-site analysis.
