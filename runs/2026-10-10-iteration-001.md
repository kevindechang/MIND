## Adversarial decision / gate
A C-vs-A gain alone demonstrates access to prior evidence, not a causal-graph mechanism. If raw transcript or three-bit memory matches C, graph necessity is unsupported in this toy family. Missing post-change diagnostic evidence makes the problem unidentifiable, not a memory failure. Proposed engineering floor C-B >= 10 percentage points with a world-clustered 95% interval excluding zero; **not preregistered**, sample size/power and model budget pending. G0 remains OPEN; no code, agent trials or data.
