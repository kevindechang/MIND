## Proposal — what should be investigated

The draft proposes a dual-rate hybrid agent combining (a) a bounded Global Workspace with explicit attention schema and causal broadcast traces, (b) Conway-inspired autobiographical organization linked to bitemporal/provenance-tracked records, (c) test-time-learning / fast-weight adaptation with slower LoRA-style consolidation, and (d) active-inference or curiosity-motivated internal maintenance. It argues that causal self-model continuity matters beyond retrieval accuracy.

These are **PROPOSED mechanisms**, not evidence that the integrated design constitutes a mind or has phenomenal consciousness. The draft's Architecture D score is an author-assigned rubric, not an empirical architectural decision; the stated weighted totals also contain arithmetic inconsistencies.

## Integration with existing hypotheses (avoid duplication)

- **H001 / E001 (current priority):** autobiographical and temporally valid memory must change downstream action, not merely retrieval answer text. Use held-out causal interventions, matched retrieval-only / sham / ablated controls. Bitemporal and aspect-preserving memory can become *competing memory variants*, but do not introduce new factors before E001 preregistration.
- **H002:** explicit self-model and metacognitive prediction of the agent's limits; distinguish operational self-model interventions from descriptive self-claims.
- **H003:** global workspace / attention-schema broadcast and winner-identity tracing. Compare against direct orchestration, static goals and matched-compute controls; ablate actual broadcast edge, not just prompt labels.
- **H005:** active inference / resource-regulation / epistemic curiosity as a candidate policy for internal computation. Compete against simpler uncertainty-reduction scheduling and deterministic priority queues; outreach boundaries are safety requirements, not consciousness evidence.
- **Separate future candidate (not yet H007):** durable plasticity under novel transfer and forgetting; compare external-state learning, procedural rule updates, test-time adaptive state, per-user low-rank adapters and combinations. Vary method at fixed memory, tokens, training FLOPs and retention probes.
- **H006 remains binding:** neither behavioral imitation, multimodule integration, self-report, nor theory-dependent indicator satisfaction alone demonstrates phenomenal experience. Track C must retain strong substrate-dependent and non-LLM counterpositions.

## Claims that MUST NOT be imported as facts

1. **"No learning without parameter updates."** Too restrictive as a definition: persistent external policy/state updates can change future behavior. Empirically compare generalization and transfer rather than assuming weights are constitutive.
2. **"Orthogonal LoRA guarantees zero interference / zero forgetting / safety."** Unsupported. Contemporary continual-learning research identifies residual interference and stability-plasticity trade-offs. Reference: [JANUS-LoRA (ICML 2026)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/chen26fz.html), peer-reviewed paper; source-level review pending.
3. **"TTT on a 3B–7B iPhone model yields p95 <=50ms."** Unsupported extrapolation. [Sun et al., TTT (ICML 2025)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/sun25h.html) reports experiments from 125M to 1.3B parameters and memory-I/O challenges; no Anjo edge runtime benchmark has been produced.
4. **"Deleting memory nodes plus adapter rollback guarantees erasure."** Operational/privacy hypothesis requiring threat model, derived artifact inventory, repeated extraction attacks and audited deletion; no blanket guarantee.
5. **"Global Workspace/AST/TTT establishes consciousness."** Theories have competing commitments; the [COGITATE 2025 Nature adversarial study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-08888-1) challenges core predictions of GNWT and IIT in humans. Those human observations do not directly validate synthetic implementations.
6. **"Proposed 2x2 experiment establishes value of each cognitive component."** The draft bundles multiple mechanisms in arms, making attribution impossible; prefer staged ablations and a budget-matched factor design.
7. **"Statutory compliance and quantitative benchmark targets are already guaranteed."** Jurisdiction-specific legal rules, precise thresholds and baseline numbers need independent validation. Regulatory safeguards must be evaluated separately from cognitive hypotheses.

### Research questions to queue *after* resolving B001

1. When does online parametric/state adaptation outperform ordinary retrieval or persistent rule-policy changes on unseen transfer?
2. Does true workspace broadcast cause cross-module integration, or merely increase context and compute?
3. Can an explicit self-model predict failures, adapt under hidden perturbation and remain calibrated across sessions?
4. What outcomes distinguish active inference from a simpler scheduler with tuned information-gain heuristics?
5. Which computational indicators can discriminate rival consciousness models, and what would still be unknowable about phenomenal experience?
