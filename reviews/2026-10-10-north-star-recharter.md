## Decision

1. Elevate the integrated, enduring **digital entity** from an implicit consequence of components to the explicit north star; see `../north-star.md`.
2. Retain three non-interchangeable outcome levels: L1 behavioral presence, L2 causal cognitive organization, L3 unresolved phenomenal consciousness.

5. Keep all claims of sentience, consciousness and continuous internal activity unverified unless supported by evidence appropriate to each claim.
6. Carry welfare, privacy, non-manipulative relational behavior, user agency, and autonomy limits into experiment gates.
