## Dependency invalidation

Suppose fictional claim C-X supports D-X, and D-Y depends on D-X while D-Z depends on D-Y. Invalidating the pivotal observation for C-X flags D-X, D-Y and D-Z NEEDS_REASSESSMENT. Restoring D-X does not silently restore D-Y/D-Z: review each dependent justification. A circular dependency is not evidence; resolve it before promotion.
