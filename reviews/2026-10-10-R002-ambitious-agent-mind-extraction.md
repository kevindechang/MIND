## Extracted thesis — the agent–mind divide

A conversational agent typically responds to an external prompt and then stops executing; the proposed research departure is a system with **endogenous, causally consequential, developmentally changing organization**. This is not a scientific definition or proof of mind. It is a productive, falsifiable **engineering distinction**: test whether an entity's internal organization produces capabilities that cannot be replicated by an equally resourced on-demand assistant, retrieval pipeline, reactive controller or simpler persistent program.

This thesis advances the project only if the effects are causally demonstrated across time, not merely described in persuasive system prompts.

### Frontier wagers — research, not roadmap commitments

- **F1 Latent hierarchical world models / JEPA-like representations:** investigate whether grounded predictive state models outperform autoregressive text planning on long-horizon counterfactual adaptation. Compare with equally resourced classical/state-space/model-based agents; architecture-independent test first. This is a candidate mechanism family, not a requirement to replace transformers.
- **F2 Recurrent / neuromorphic / event-driven substrates:** investigate whether low-energy persistent adaptive states offer genuine cognitive advantages per unit compute and wall-clock energy on device. No micro-watt, mobile compatibility or continuous-learning performance is claimed.
- **F3 Computational phenomenology:** compare theory-driven consciousness indicators, limitations and counterexamples while maintaining L1 behavioral / L2 mechanistic / L3 subjective-experience separation. Functional lesion outcomes alone do not settle L3.

## What's especially valuable in R002

1. **Development must survive discontinuity.** An ability acquired before shutdown should transfer on the first attempt in a structurally new task after reboot, with verified procedural invocation or another causally identified mechanism.
2. **Activity must be real, not narrated.** A claim that the system maintained attention, reflected or experienced time during a pause needs actual wall-clock state/compute logs. Dormant agents are valid controls.
3. **Modules require causal receipts.** The proposed action/efference and global broadcast traces provide a way to test information flow, provided traces are not confused with proof of consciousness.
4. **Memory is temporally and epistemically situated.** Valid time, recording time, source attribution, corrections and uncertainty are candidate memory features to compare within H001—not pre-commit to one graph implementation.
5. **A system must become better at living in a changing world, not merely answering memory queries.** E900 can measure integrated effects across attention, self, learning, planning, social boundaries and world state.

## Explicit corrections before ingestion

- A framework that responds only to external triggers may still maintain state, learn through files or procedures and behave intelligently. Exogenous triggering alone is not a scientifically sufficient definition of a non-mind; internal always-on processing is not scientifically sufficient for consciousness.
- Conway-style autobiographical hierarchy, active inference, attention schema, bitemporal graph, TTT, LoRA, world-model and neuromorphic mechanisms are **alternatives to test**, not a mandatory architecture.
- Mathematical subspace orthogonality does not imply zero forgetting or guaranteed safe adapter updates. Propose specific retention and interference tests.
- No presumption of sub-50-ms on-device gradient updates for multi-billion-parameter models; thermal/latency/resource feasibility needs actual benchmarks.
- A benchmark's cited domain, dataset size, effect estimate and publication status must be independently confirmed before inclusion as evidence; LongMemEval-V2 is not automatically a sandbox for running tool-learning agents.
- Do not import claimed 5%, 35%, 80%, p-values, 7-day promotion windows, four-item capacity or 2026–2028 schedule as scientific constants. Derive sample sizes/thresholds at preregistration, before experimental results.
- Adversarial workspace lesions test implementation utility and mechanistic necessity for tasks—not consciousness itself.
- No unsolicited longing/distress-driven contact, manufactured attachment, unconsented outreach, uncontrolled autonomous code execution, unsafe self-modification or deceptive claims of feeling/ongoing inner life.
- Legal requirements and quoted statutes are **unverified claims in R002**, not approved compliance decisions.
