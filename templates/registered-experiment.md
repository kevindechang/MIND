## Stage 1 — before any scored trial
1. Scientific question and explicit comparison with strongest simpler rival.
2. Target population/world family, inclusion/exclusion, consent/welfare check.
3. Formal transition model, action/observation semantics and identifiable oracle/uncertainty.
4. Models, tools, memory/interface, prompt and retrieval controls; match tokens/calls/FLOPs/time/cost as predeclared or justify differences.
5. Experimental arms: no-memory, retrieval/raw history, proposed mechanism, sham, ablation, compact state, other competitors.
6. Independent world/seed randomization, held-out mechanism split and information leakage protection.
7. Primary and secondary outcomes, effect size, CI/test, multiplicity, sample size/power or precision and stopping rules.
8. Falsifiers, null/negative interpretation and protocol amendments policy.
9. Code/environment hash, review/approval, resources and run approval gate.

## Stage 2 — after execution, kept separate
1. Freeze reference and exact deviation log (if any).
2. Time, executor, commands, stdout/log paths, seeds, versions, hashes and costs.
3. Run accounting, failures, excluded cases with predeclared reasons.
4. Primary effect and uncertainty, secondary, negative controls, nulls and ablations.
5. Reproduction instructions and independent verification status.
6. Limitations, architecture consequence, E900 and L1/L2/L3 separation.

An absence of executed agent trials must be written explicitly, not shown as an empty results table.

## Compounding transfer fields

- Stable decision ID/claim anchor; pivotal evidence version and strongest rival.
- Outcome → action mapping, scoped support and uncertainty; null is not equivalence.
- Prerequisite/dependent decision IDs, freshness and transitive reassessment if evidence fails.
- Reused asset ID/version, assumptions, verification provenance and revalidation.
- Proposed E900 observable/cross-function intervention; component win is not integration or L3.
- Work kind, scientific verdict and distinct contribution ID; amendments do not multiply acceptance.
- Attention eligibility vs accepted-science eligibility under [work accounting](../research-records.md).
- Exactly one next executable action, required tools/inputs/deliverable/stop condition.
