# Scientific Syntheses and Cross-Stream Decisions

**Purpose:** periodically reconcile living dossiers, actual experiments and competing architecture proposals into a falsifiable scientific program. Do not write an 'ultimate verdict' merely because many pages exist.

## Trigger
After eight new distinct valid accepted contributions, or earlier for a major validated finding/pivotal dependency invalidation. Use [work/milestone accounting](../research-records.md), not the legacy stored-iteration number. Inspect W1–W4 and E900 together, including absent/contrary evidence, dependency freshness and reviewer provenance. Reaccepting/amending a contribution cannot duplicate a milestone. Governance is not accepted science.

## Current baseline
[000-program-baseline.md](000-program-baseline.md) is a retrospective inventory of status and missing evidence, **not** a completed cross-stream scientific review or new result.

## Minimum synthesis
- What hypotheses gained/lost support by *new* evidence (not just argument)?
- What precise method, environment, and independent replication justify it?
- Which contradictions or null results survive, what competing explanation remains?
- Which expensive architectures remain unselected, what ablations would eliminate them?
- Are L1, L2 and L3 still distinguished? E900 P0/P1/P2 statuses updated only from actual observations.
- What single highest-value blocker follows, and why does it beat alternatives?
- Who reviewed the synthesis? Real independent subagent vs SELF-REVIEW_ONLY.

Use [publication criteria](../publication-standard.md) and [synthesis template](../templates/scientific-synthesis.md).
