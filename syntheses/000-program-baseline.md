**Date:** 2026-10-10 · Type: RETROSPECTIVE STATUS BASELINE · NOT an eighth-iteration audit · NO NEW EXPERIMENTAL EVIDENCE.

The Digital Mind program seeks an enduring, developing digital entity with distinct L1 behavior, L2 causal functional organization, and unresolved L3 phenomenal consciousness. Current [canonical state](../research-state.md) records latest verified iteration **003**, E001 G0 OPEN, candidate A0/A1 architectures and E900 only proposed.

## What is observed in the project's records
Iterations 001–003 establish an E001 causal-memory test proposal and two deterministic toy instruments. XOR Switchboard reveals full identifiability from three diagnostic events; Causal Commitments exposes the action-effect matrix from complete interventions. Local simulator unit-test and enumeration results are logged in [E001 methodology](../experiments/E001/methodology.md) and [results-status register](../experiments/E001/results.md). These checks support instrument correctness on sampled/enumerated cases, not any claim that an AI agent learned a mind-like causal self.

## The strongest disconfirming mechanism
An information-sufficient compact state, raw event trace or predictive representation can in principle preserve all control-relevant information in these constructed worlds. Thus the desired complex autobiographical graph has **no demonstrated necessity** in the current test family. Further complexity is justified only by a budget- and evidence-matched advantage in richer unknown/partial environments.

## What is still unobserved
No logged model-arm experiment against competing memories; no rigorous partial observability protocol; no actual recurrent or multi-agent integrated mind deployment; no human-subject/social evaluation; no longitudinal E900 P1/P2 time; no evidence of subjective experience. External neuroscience and AI benchmark papers in the evidence ledger are literature, not independently reproduced project results.
