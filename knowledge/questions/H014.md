**Question (opened by C030, C033, C034 and C036).** The workspace delivers the same few contents to every organ, and every consumer takes everything it is given. Shadows show what that costs. In vigil, the self-model forecasts better *without* memory's contents. They are informative (real beats a sham that keeps presence and feature load fixed; C036), but carrying their features dilutes its normalised learning more than they inform it (C030). The policy acts worse when it receives the self-model's contents (C034). The kernel's shuffle sham of them costs it about two thirds as much, which suggests feature load rather than misinformation, but that sham does not hold presence and load fixed. Yet in silent outages a single memory content serves both control and metacognition (C033). In this organism and these worlds, broadcast access helps a consumer that can use a content and costs one that cannot ignore it.

Global-workspace accounts describe specialised processors that take from the broadcast what they can use. The default organism's consumers have no such receiver-side selection; the only selection happens at the workspace (H003). The question is whether receiver-side selection keeps the gains of global availability without its costs, and whether the selection must be learned.

**Strongest rival:** a fixed per-consumer filter chosen with the same information (for example, the best subset of sources for each consumer, picked on dev seeds). If it matches the learned gate, learning adds nothing. A gate that wins only because it was given the answer (e.g. hard-coded to drop memory in vigil) is by construction and must be labelled so.

Scope: L2, this organism and these worlds. Related: [H003](H003.md) (sender-side selection), [H007](H007.md) (integration), [H013](H013.md) (self-reports that reach action).

endpoint. [K14](../claims/K14_selective_self_model_replay.py) validates replay for all
21 configurations. These results are specific to the implemented mixture, not every
possible receiver-selection mechanism. The policy's carry cost and live behavioural

and [sealed protocol](../../docs/H014-selective-integration-protocol.md).
