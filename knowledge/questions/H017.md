**Hypothesis.** H016 found that exact inference over a generic hypothesis space of causal graphs makes routed information
seeking pay on the uniform prior (C085, C089), and does no harm under the skewed prior (C087). It did not remove the loss under
censored probes (C086). The diagnosis (exploration) was that the prior is the problem, not the value rule. Under the uniform
prior over 25 DAGs, node 2 can have outgoing edges, so its reach looks uncertain, and the organism keeps probing it. In the
workshop, node 2 is always a sink. So the organism wastes probes on a node it could learn is uninformative. The hypothesis: an
organism that learns the prior over structures from its own probes, by online EM, concentrates the prior on the structures its
experience supports. That removes the wasted probes, and it should help where the uniform prior hurt.

**What the organism is given, and what it learns.** The hypothesis space (25 labelled DAGs on three nodes, the reach semantics)
is still supplied. The prior over that space starts uniform. After each episode, the organism computes the responsibilities of
the DAGs that explain the probe results it learned in that episode, accumulates them, and uses the normalised totals as the prior
for the next episode. The eight forward-only graphs of the workshop are not supplied.

**Exploration that motivated the claims (dev seeds 0-7, 1500 episodes; not evidence).**

1. *The learned prior recovers the family.* Starting uniform (8 of 25 graphs are forward-only, so 0.32 of the mass), the learned
   prior put 0.85 to 0.86 of its mass on the forward-only family on dev, and about 0.6 under censoring, where fewer probe
   results are learned.
2. *Probes fall and utility holds on dev.* Dev: probes 0.63 against 0.88 with the uniform prior, utility -1.40 against -1.39.
3. *Censored and skewed probes gain.* Censored: utility -1.65 against -1.69 (plain -1.67). Skewed: -1.45 against -1.48 (plain -1.46).
4. *Diagnostic against the Bayes reference.* Under censoring the Bayes reference probes both nodes and retries after censored
   results. The package probed less and wasted probes on node 2. The learned prior removes most of the node-2 waste.

**Design choices, fixed before registration.** Online EM over the 25 DAGs, Laplace smoothing alpha 0.1, updated at each episode
boundary from that episode's facts, with the accumulated responsibilities kept across episodes. The rest of the package is H016's
(censor-aware value, value carried as salience, value-scaled route at 4.0), unchanged. The held-out seeds are drawn from the drand
beacon after the claim commit is pushed.

**Margins.** C090: mean forward-family mass at least 0.80 (from a uniform start of 0.32). C091: probes fewer by more than 0.10
per episode. C092: utility no lower than the uniform prior by more than 0.05 (noninferiority). C093: utility higher than the uniform
prior under censoring, margin zero (the direction only). C094 and C095: utility no lower than plain by more than 0.05 under censoring
and skew (noninferiority, the same margin as H016's equivalence claims, now one-sided).

**Limits, stated before any held-out run.**

- The hypothesis space is supplied: three nodes, DAGs, and the reach semantics of the world. Learning the space itself is not
  tested here.
- The prior is learned from probes the organism chose, which may bias it in ways the exact Bayes posterior is not. The forward-family
  mass is a direct measure of what it learned, but it is not a proof that the learning is unbiased.
- The EM uses the same one-step value rule as H016. The gap to the Bayes reference is partly non-myopic planning.
- Censored and skewed results are still exploration-based predictions for the learned prior; C093-C095 test them on fresh seeds.
- No correction for multiple comparisons across C090-C095.

**Strongest rivals.** The uniform prior with the same route (C091-C093); the plain organism (C094, C095); H016's package; and the
Bayes reference (`rival:bayes`), a ceiling that is not required to be beaten.

**Why C094 and C095 are not PREREGISTERED.** They carry `about=("causal",)`, so the rival rule applied. The compact rival
(`organism +causal causal.derive=0`, facts without the derived plan, no value of information, no efference record) was not beaten on
either variant. On dev the difference from the compact rival is -0.001 under censoring and +0.002 under skew, with intervals of
about +/-0.04. On held-out it is +0.035 and +0.025, with intervals that include zero. A noninferiority claim against plain should not
have been registered with the rival rule attached. The claims cannot be changed after their held-out run, so a successor with
`about` empty is the honest route.

**What this shows.** The learned prior recovers the workshop's family from the organism's own experience, from a uniform start, on
held-out seeds (C090). Learning it makes the organism buy about 0.25 fewer probes per episode without losing utility on the uniform
prior (C091, C092). On the uniform prior the full package beats the facts-only compact rival (C079, C085, C089; see H015 and H016).

**What it does not show, and the boundary this draws.** Under censored probes (C093) and under the skewed prior (C094, C095), the
full package is no better than the facts-only compact organism: its gain over plain is shared with a facts-only organism. So the value
of information and the routing add nothing beyond the facts in those two regimes, and the learned prior's advantage under censoring is
not established on held-out seeds. The mechanism that the programme has confirmed is specific to the uniform prior with complete
information.
