**Hypothesis (v1 H010, from R002).** Explicit predictive traces of one's own actions improve causal attribution between self-induced and exogenous changes. Note the trap v1 hit repeatedly: if the world hands the agent an "external" label, attribution is by construction; the world must make the confound real.

**2026-10-11.** `poltergeist` aliases a forced push or pull with a pre-drawn external impulse in one clipped sensation. K12 checks that no single transition reveals polarity or the external label, including with the actuator working, and that private causes stay out of the observation. C037: a privileged filter that keeps a running prediction of the act beats a moment judge with the same parameters and the same current act and sensation, by more than 0.05 (held-out Δ +0.081 [+0.074, +0.088]). C038: the optional `efference` organ beats `rival:estimator` (the organism's Q-learner on the same public observations and own acts, no forward model) by more than 0.04 (held-out Δ +0.063 [+0.055, +0.071]). The same runs do not beat the privileged filter; it stays a ceiling. One contributor, one environment. Not a phenomenal claim. The act is the only legal action on the act step, so this is attribution given the act, not a test of whether the organism chooses to move. Gusty and slippy are not in C037 or C038.
