**A pipeline question, not a hypothesis about minds.** Commit-sealed seeds stop inadvertent tuning and post-hoc
prediction edits. They do not stop deliberate grinding: a registrant can preview candidate commit hashes offline and
commit the one whose seeds favour them (v2 review, finding 2a). Closing this needs an external source of
randomness or a second party. Until then it is forbidden in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and undetectable by the tool.

**Mechanism (2026-10-12).** [mind/beacon.py](../../mind/beacon.py): seeds = sealed seeds keyed additionally by the
randomness of the first drand quicknet round after the earliest GitHub activity-log push containing the seal.
GitHub's clock timestamps the push and the beacon supplies randomness unknown at that moment, so offline previewing
is impossible. Not verified: the drand BLS signature (stdlib only; `--online` trusts the relays). Not prevented, only
made visible: pushing several registrations and keeping the luckiest.
