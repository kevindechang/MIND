**Question (from v1 E100 Day 6, PR #11).** When the organism reports on itself, is the report grounded in the states
that cause its behaviour, or is it a plausible narrative? v1's liar-twin instrument ran a grounded ledger reader and a
zero-read narrator on the same policy. The grounded reader acted better (3 vs 1), yet a constructed preference metric
favoured the narrator on every probe. A metric that rewards how a report reads cannot tell grounded reports from
confabulated ones.

**v2 operationalisation.** A report is valid to the extent that it predicts the organism's next action better than a
report built without access to its internal state, and to the extent that lesions move report and action together.
The organism already broadcasts its intentions (`pending=` from goals) and its capability forecasts (`self:` from the
self-model). Scoring the self-model's forecasts against outcomes (C017, C018) is a narrow case of this question. Access
lesions (`cut:X>Y`) separate what is reportable (in the workspace) from what reaches the policy. Memory and goals are
degenerate (C004), so a cut to one path may be compensated by the other: cut both when testing for dissociation.

**Strongest rival:** a narrator that sees the same transcript as the organism but none of its internal state. If it
predicts the next action as well as the grounded report, the report adds nothing. Scope: L2, meaning the coupling
between report and cause in this organism. It says nothing about experience ([H006](H006.md)).

**What was tested.** [C019](../claims/C019_report_beats_narrator.py) and
[C020](../claims/C020_report_validity_follows_the_cut.py) score a fixed readout of the broadcast
(`pending=`, else `was:request=`, when `bell=1` is broadcast; `self:` labels are confidence, not an action)
against `rival:narrator`, which remembers the public `request` for the whole episode and never sees the
broadcast. Both are scored only on late-half bell ticks in vigil/dev, by agreement with the action taken.
Margins were set before the sealed runs: 0.10 for the narrator comparison, 0.25 for the accuracy drop, and a
reportability rule that the cut must leave an intention token on at least half of those ticks and must not
lower that rate by 0.15 or more.

C019 is REFUTED on dev and on its first sealed held-out run. The narrator is more accurate (dev 0.915 vs
0.838, difference −0.077 [−0.127, −0.027]; held-out 0.905 vs 0.814, difference −0.090 [−0.131, −0.050]). On
this organism, in this world, the direct intention tokens do not predict the next action better than the
public transcript. Often the workspace holds `self:` doubt tags instead of `pending=`, while the policy
still performs the requested action.

C020 holds on dev and on its first sealed held-out run (PREREGISTERED). The cut is
`cut:goals>policy cut:memory>policy cut:self_model>policy`. Memory and goals are both cut because they are
degenerate (C004). The self-model is cut as well: with only the first two cuts, doubt tags still reach the
policy and accuracy does not reliably fall, so that lesion does not remove the broadcast path. Perception is
not cut. Accuracy falls from 0.838 to 0.033 on dev (drop +0.805 [+0.746, +0.864]) and from 0.803 to 0.039 on
held-out (drop +0.764 [+0.688, +0.840]). Reportability does not fall (dev 0.891 → 0.975; held-out 0.879 →
0.966). The report still names the intention; the action no longer follows it.
