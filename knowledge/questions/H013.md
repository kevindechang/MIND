Earlier v1 liar-twin work showed that a preference metric can reward an ungrounded narrator. Report validity here is measured only by prequential action prediction, never text quality.

**Hypothesis C023:** a readout using the organism's selected broadcast plus public transcript improves multiclass Brier action-prediction loss by **>0.02** over a transcript-only narrator in **both** vigil/long and vigil/crowded. Each variant uses 24 independent lifetimes of 180 episodes: 120 online training, then 60 frozen test episodes. **Mechanism:** pending intentions and remembered requests can enter the workspace and influence policy. The grounded readout can observe selected broadcast tags; the narrator must infer them from public history. Both use the same online softmax model, see identical public observations, actions, outcomes and rewards, and are scored on the **same** actions. The narrator sees the entire current episode transcript and lifetime public count summaries, not a zero-input strawman.
