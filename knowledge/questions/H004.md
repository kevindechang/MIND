**Hypothesis (v1 H004).** Recurrent state yields stronger temporal integration and counterfactual update than equally resourced feed-forward deliberation over longer context. The standing `window4` and `window8` rivals are not by themselves sufficient to establish this: longer temporal access and model capacity are potential confounds.

## Preregistered design — 2026-10-11 (H004 sprint)

**Mechanism.** Add an optional, fixed-width recurrent trace to the *shared* organism. Each tick its previous state competes for workspace access; its update is based only on attended public percepts and the previous action's already-public outcome. Use a deterministic, source-neutral signed feature projection and exponential recurrent update, not special handling of `request`, `bell`, `scan`, or any scorer-only world information. It learns a changing state online; the projection itself is not trained. Calling it a trained recurrent representation would overstate the mechanism.

**Primary hypothesis / claim C021.** In `vigil/long`, adding recurrent state to the unmodified organism improves the fraction of fulfilled commitments by more than **0.05** over 300 episodes, averaged across **16 paired lifetimes**. The 0.05 margin means at least one extra correct commitment per twenty episodes and is selected **before** new dev runs, not fitted to results. C021 is falsified if the conservative interval fails to clear +0.05; an interval that straddles +0.05 is inconclusive, not proof of no effect. Because goals already retain intentions, a null effect is plausible and scientifically valuable.

**Cross-world hypothesis / claim C022.** In `outage/silent`, the same recurrent state improves mean reward/episode by **0.10** over the identical organism without it, across **16 paired lifetimes**. A failure is independently reported, never suppressed by C021. The comparison with no recurrent organ isolates the added mechanism, but does not prove optimality, integration, or consciousness.

**Strong simple rivals.** Long raw-transcript TD policies, especially `window16` and `window32`, with the same TD policy family as the organism. The matched-ablation organism is the critical causal rival; transcript baselines reveal whether a simpler representation does just as well or better. Longer windows have greater memory and feature-combination cost, so they are not parameter-matched; measure that limitation rather than claiming equivalent compute. The privileged Bayes oracle remains a ceiling, not a rival the organism must beat.

**By-construction / leakage falsifiers.** Recurrent features must not access world `info`, future observations, the chosen current action, policy weights or outcome of that action. Disabling recurrent proposal or cutting `recurrent>policy` should remove its direct policy pathway; if the apparent gain remains under those conditions, it cannot be credited solely to the action pathway. Verify hash-seed-invariant ledger replay and that the same external observation history yields the same recurrent state; avoid recurrent self-feedback through its own proposed contents.

**Exploration versus confirmation.** Dev seeds may be explored before claims are committed; claim source must be committed before any held-out run. Every dev/held-out record, including negative results, must be committed without editing its prediction. The second world does not rescue failure in the first.
