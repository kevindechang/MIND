**Methodological constraint, not a testable claim.** v2 enforces it mechanically: claim `scope` may be `instrument`, `L1` or `L2`; the doctor rejects anything else. Atlas measures (causal density, cross-function effects, degeneracy) describe causal organisation; they are not consciousness scores. See [docs/PIPELINE.md](../../docs/PIPELINE.md).
