**Hypothesis (v1 H008).** Internally maintained temporal state yields distinctive, useful prospective action even without recent interaction. Partial v2 evidence: in `vigil`, persisted intentions (goals) and memory carry a commitment across a gap (C001), and the whole learned organism continues byte-identically across real process death, including after a durable commit (K01, K02, K06, K07). Endogenous *maintenance* (choosing what to recompute when idle) is not yet implemented.
