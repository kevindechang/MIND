**Hypothesis (v1 H002).** An explicit model of one's own capabilities and uncertainty improves calibration and self-correction after hidden capability changes, beyond more compute or a static self-description.

**v2 operationalisation.** The `self_model` organ fits P(success | action, broadcast context) online and reports forecasts that are scored against public outcomes (Brier). Its standing simple rival is a per-action success table (the transcript rivals' forecaster), the rival that v1 PR #15 showed ties a hand-written "self-model" exactly. C007 (the self-model beats the table when outages are announced) and C008 (it merely ties the table when they are silent) test it on the shared organism: the advantage is entirely the information in context.
