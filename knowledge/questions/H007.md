**Hypothesis (v1 H007).** An agent whose goals, self-model, memory and world model are causally linked shows adaptation and intervention-resilience beyond independent modules.

**v2 operationalisation: the integration atlas** (`python -m mind atlas`). For every organ × competency in every world it estimates the lesion effect. Integration is visible as (a) *cross-function* effects — an organ reliably affecting a competency it was not designed for — and (b) *degeneracy* — pairs whose joint lesion hurts more than the sum of singles (mutual backup). A purely modular system shows neither. Claims C002–C004 (context-dependent necessity and memory/goals degeneracy), C009–C010 (memory helps control but costs calibration), C013 (self-knowledge improves causal decisions) and C014 (atlas-level integration) test it.
