The compact facts-only rival was beaten by C079, C080 and C081 on dev, and the held-out PREREGISTERED state requires the
rival panel to be clean on the held-out seeds too.

**What the confirmation does and does not show.** It shows that, in the uniform, uncensored workshop, a value-of-information
organ fed by an efference-style record and routed to action reaches regret 0.33 on fresh seeds, against 0.56 for the plain
organism, and that the route is needed. It does not show transfer: under censored probes it harms the organism, and under the
skewed prior it gains little. Those boundaries are the next question.

**Confound found after registration, and how it was checked (exploration, not evidence; dev seeds 0-7).** The efference
arm reads commitments from the observation, but the workspace-fed arm sees both commitments on the same tick in only 28% of
decisions. So C080 could have measured commitment access, not probe-result access. A decomposition crossing the two channels
settles it:

| probes | commitments | regret | utility |
|---|---|---|---|
| observation (efference) | observation | 0.320 | −1.438 |
| observation (efference) | workspace | 0.317 | −1.435 |
| workspace | observation | 0.702 | −1.820 |
| workspace | workspace | 0.702 | −1.820 |

The probe channel carries the whole effect. The commitment channel does not, because a commitment, once learned in an episode,
stays available to the organ. C080's claim is therefore about the probe-result record. Formal confirmation needs a split-source
option in the repo and a registered equivalence claim, which is the next step.
**Hypothesis.** A limited-capacity global workspace decides which experiences an organism learns from. A learned model of
the world built only from the experiences that win the workspace is sparse and biased. Information seeking is a decision
about what to learn, and it needs two things: a learned prior over what unseen structure could be, and a direct route from
the proposal "probing node n is worth it" to action. A learner that gets the proposal only through learned weights will
not act on it, because it has rarely taken the action.

**Exploration that motivated the claims (dev seeds 0-7, 1500 episodes; not evidence).**

1. *Attention biases the learned model.* For node 0, the attended record learned 67 of 590 probe results (11%), and its
   learned distribution of reach sets was 0.108 away from the truth (total variation). The efference record (the organ's
   own probe observations, outside the workspace) learned 308 of 329 results and was 0.021 away.
2. *The route is necessary.* With the efference record and value-of-information probing, the probe recommendation
   changed almost nothing (0.17 probes per episode, regret 0.59) until it was routed to action with a bonus. Routed, probes
   went to 0.87 per episode and regret fell to 0.32, against 0.56 for the plain organism.
3. *The attention-fed version is worse than plain.* Routed and attention-fed: regret 0.70. The bias costs more than the
   route gains.
4. *Robustness.* The routed efference organism stayed at regret 0.30-0.36 for route bonuses of 0.5, 1.0 and 2.0.
5. *Boundaries.* With censored probes (half of them return nothing), the routed organism was worse (regret 0.44 against
   0.36): the value estimate assumes every probe informs. Under the skewed prior the plain organism is already good
   (regret 0.34), so the routed organism gained little.
6. *Sink node.* Node 2's reach is always {2}. In the second half of a life the plain organism spends 56% of its probes on
   it. The value-of-information organ's prior learns that node 2 is a sink, and it recommends a probe of node 2 only 3 times
   across the second halves of four lives.

**Design choices, fixed before registration.** Salience 2.0 (inherited from H001). Probe cost 0.25, which is the workshop's
cost and is supplied as domain knowledge. Smoothing 1.0 for the counts. Bonus 1.0, checked at 0.5 and 2.0 on the same seeds.
The held-out seeds are drawn from the drand beacon after the claim commit is pushed, so they are independent of everything
above.

**Margins.** 0.15 utility for the component comparisons (C079-C081). 0.42 regret for C082, which is a quarter of the gap
between the plain organism (about 0.56) and the Bayes optimum. 0.05 and 0.10 for the boundary claims C083 and C084, which
are registered so that the limits are recorded whichever way they fall.

**Limits, stated before any held-out run.**

- The organ's causal model is supplied: XOR composition, the penalty and the probe cost are the workshop's rule. The eight
  graphs stay hidden, but the scoring rule is built in. These claims test a learned prior and calibrated probing on top of
  that rule, not causal discovery from scratch.
- The efference record is an assumption about architecture. It is an organ that reads the observation of its own probes
  outside the workspace. C080 tests whether that assumption matters, against the attention-fed version. It does not test
  whether the brain has such a record.
- The value-of-information estimate treats the unknown nodes' priors as independent, except for a chained pair count. It is
  a one-step approximation, not the exact Bayes value of information.
- The prior is estimated from probes the organism chose adaptively. A probe of node 1 may be more likely once node 0's
  result is known, so the counts for node 1 are not a random sample of graphs. The exact Bayes posterior does not have this
  problem. The uncensored held-out gains show the bias does not destroy the benefit here, but it is a limit on any claim
  about calibration.
- No correction for multiple comparisons across C079-C084.

**Strongest rivals.** `rival:bayes` (privileged ceiling, not required to be beaten); the compact facts-only rival
(`organism +causal causal.derive=0`), which C079 and the other claims also face when they hold; and the same organism
without the route (C081).
