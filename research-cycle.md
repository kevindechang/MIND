## 1. Select a ready decision and PLAN

Choose one W1–W4 question with satisfied prerequisites and the best qualitative decision impact, uncertainty reduction, integration relevance, cost and feasibility. E001/D001 is the initial priority, not a prohibition on ready independent work. Check [allocation](research-records.md#allocation-and-synthesis).

Before work, record:

- stable decision ID or a concrete new decision record; claim scope and current freshness;
- strongest viable simpler rival, genuinely discriminating predictions and outcome → action mapping;
- falsifier, estimand, measurements, observations, identifiability, confounds and negative controls;
- primary methods and counterevidence to inspect; disclose source dependence;
- reusable asset ID/version, applicable assumptions, modifications and revalidation needed;
- smallest deliverable and its dependencies; lifecycle information/compute/tool/memory budgets;
- frozen scored criteria where applicable, approvals, ethics and interruption checkpoint;
- how an outcome would change an E900 observable or cross-function intervention.

After three substantive attempts on a blocker without gate movement, resolved uncertainty or changed decision, reassess before attempt four. Simplify the test, resolve a capability, switch to a ready dependency, retire an invalid benchmark or justify genuinely new information gain. Reference the previous attempts; renaming/padding cannot reset them.

For scoped research already authorized by the charter, execute a bounded plan with actually available methods/tools. New architecture adoption, product behavior, personal data access, autonomy or spending retain their approval gates.

## 2. Investigate or build the discriminating test

Retrieve direct primary-source versions/dates and inspect the relevant methods, assumptions, baselines, findings and limitations. Abstract-only evidence generates leads, not a full-depth empirical conclusion. Preserve contrary evidence and null findings. Do not pool dependent studies as independent replications.

Use explicit research design/planning, test-first development for executable code and systematic debugging for failures. Separate calibration/pilot development from held-out scored trials; preregister before scoring. Record exact commands, versions, logs, costs and real process reset. A deterministic oracle/unit check is an instrument result, not an agent mechanism result.

D001's next design work must distinguish identifiable from observationally ambiguous histories and use a permitted-history oracle. Private simulator truth is diagnostic only. Freeze E001 resource/analysis choices before G2. If model execution is unavailable, name that block and do ready design work without buying an API or inventing a result.

## 3. Review scientific validity and dependencies

Apply [review-protocol](review-protocol.md), the quality gate and evidence-based verification before completion claims. Independent review exists only after a real distinct reviewer is invoked and the report read; otherwise SELF-REVIEW_ONLY. Require a credible falsifying rival, not simply a self-critic persona.

Audit causal identification, information/lifecycle resource parity, preregistration, uncertainty and negative controls; keep L1/L2/L3 separate. If pivotal evidence fails, flag direct and transitive dependent decisions/assets NEEDS_REASSESSMENT. Do not promote claims relying on unresolved stale premises. Preserve old findings and explain any alternate justification.

## 4. Decide, transfer and account

Choose ACCEPT / REVISE / REJECT / DEFER with bounded scope, confidence and evidence. A nonsignificant difference is not equivalence. A null, negative, theoretical or critical synthesis contribution may qualify if it actually satisfies the scientific gate; a scaffold alone does not.

Record kind and scientific verdict separately. A valid partial checkpoint may be persisted without advancing G gates. Use the [work index](research-records.md) to maintain unique IDs, accepted-contribution IDs and attention allocation. Historical 004 stays MAJOR_REVISION_REQUIRED; legacy persistence is not acceptance. Amendments do not multiply one contribution.

Update the relevant dossier/experiment when warranted, hypothesis/evidence only for justified changes, decision outcome/freshness and reusable-asset assumptions. Name the E900 implication and intervention without claiming integration. No warranted dossier change is an honest outcome; do not pad it.

## 5. Persist and hand off

Read live main before publishing; append a collision-free work artifact and update index/decisions/assets as needed. Commit artifacts first, then write research-state LAST referencing that immutable artifact commit; verify final publication by read-back. If a conflict occurs, reread/reconcile, never force-push. If interruption leaves committed artifacts ahead of state, finalize once from the index rather than rerunning or double-counting. If writes fail, NOT_COMMITTED with full delta; canonical counts unchanged.

Return: decision/blocker/gate, source inspection depth, discriminating rival, actual execution, falsifier, reviewer provenance, scientific verdict, decision delta, asset reuse, stale dependencies, persistence SHA and **one next executable action** (inputs, required tool, deliverable and stopping condition). Measure start/end/time only if actually observed; otherwise UNKNOWN. Forty minutes remains aspirational; no artificial waiting.

Deep synthesis follows eight new valid accepted contributions or a major validated finding/pivotal invalidation, using milestone contribution IDs. Allocation uses substantive attention records; governance and skipped invocations count toward neither. This governance workflow creates no research result, continuously running entity or scheduled-tool guarantee.

## 7. Short-run and deep-sprint reporting (2026-10-11 correction)

Prior manual work RN-D001-2026-10-11-001 was a valid methodological **RESEARCH_NOTE**, but a roughly nine-minute invocation did **not** constitute a completed high-effort research sprint. Never call a committed calibration exercise a completed deep-research cycle just because its assertions passed. The hourly task now attempts a cumulative multi-invocation scientific sprint, with a target of 40 minutes of actual research work **only where the execution runtime allows**. The scheduler does not guarantee any minimum execution length.

Each invocation must distinguish (1) repository transaction completion, (2) checkpoint/subtask completion, (3) completed scientific analysis or experiment satisfying its specific quality gate. If under 40 minutes observed wall-clock, mark `SHORT_RUN / DURATION_NOT_ENFORCED` and describe the incomplete components of the sprint even if an intermediate note was published. Do not convert elapsed wall-clock to asserted active-work time, do not idle to accumulate minutes, and do not conceal short duration. If duration is unknown, mark UNKNOWN without inventing a 40-minute claim.

Depth, not time alone, controls scientific completion: first inspect conflicting primary methods and explicit alternatives; formalize causal/information constraints and falsifiers; implement a discriminating test if feasible; test negative controls, leakage and failure cases; scrutinize model/prior assumptions, uncertainty and resource parity; and document a decision-changing result or preserve an unfinished checkpoint. Source lists, word counts and unit tests are insufficient on their own. Record precise accomplished phases and next unmet phase, so the next hourly invocation continues the same unresolved blocker without redundant research. Do not rewrite v0.1 when v0.2 is the next identified work.


## 8. Failure-corrected protocol and anti-loop policy (effective 2026-10-11)

This section supersedes outdated §6 instructions where they imply endless E001 oracle construction. Full operational instructions: [RUNBOOK.md](RUNBOOK.md). 1. **Restore new authority:** MIND main root, exact HEAD, uncommitted/pending handoff, migrated origin and artifact SHAs. Research records remain historical, not reset by repository relocation.
2. **Preflight actual code:** from a byte-exact checkout, run `python scripts/verify_instruments.py` (all E001, legacy and W2 tests, including the formerly missed 14+20+12+10 suites). Distinguish tests actually run now from old reports. If a checkout/runner is inaccessible, record NOT_EXECUTED and the exact missing capability. Do not use a compatibility fixture as proof of integration.
3. **Correct attention imbalance:** the next substantive research record must focus on a ready W2–W4 question (W2/E002a is ready for methodological advancement), unless a documented execution-critical failure makes this impossible. Historical 004 is NOT science accepted and does not satisfy a new attention allocation.
4. **Prioritize falsification and stronger rivals:** state the hard null, expected opposing observations, identical budget/information interface, seeds, public/private separation and process-resumption protocol *before* scoring. A compact belief policy or externally learned value-of-computation controller may match or outperform the candidate; that is an informative outcome.
5. **Stop instrument churn:** no E001 v0.5 or E002a toy sequel merely for test counts. Advancing the same blocker requires a named changed estimand, proved limitation with constructive counterexample, integrated regression fix, preregistered comparison or new decision-moving evidence. Otherwise switch ready streams. Explicitly track attempt/stall count.
6. **Time is not a gate:** seek substantive depth while runtime permits; log measured start/end if available, actual accomplished steps and skipped tasks. Mark SHORT_RUN under 40 observed wall-clock minutes; mark duration UNKNOWN if unmeasured. Never idle or fabricate 40 minutes, model agents, background computation, held-out results or peer review.
7. **Persist without inflation:** only verified substantive artifacts become records; archive failures and nulls accurately; commit artifact/record/index first, state last, read back. No new Actions, external paid services or production Anjo changes. Maintain L1/L2/L3 distinctions.
