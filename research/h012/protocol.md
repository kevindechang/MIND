# H012 switchback protocol — DESIGN ONLY

Hypothesis C024: a cue-conditioned episodic outcome table outperforms the existing organism on the first action after an A-B-A reversal by >0.15 absolute success probability, paired 24 seeds and 160 episodes, dev variant. This is a scientifically meaningful difference of 15 successes per 100 returns.

Mechanism: six A trials, twelve interfering B trials, then six return-to-A trials. Each episode draws a fresh hidden A action; B requires the opposite action. The same public phase cue appears in A1 and A2. The rival retains four outcome counts, two cues times two actions, reset each episode. Both agents receive the same public observations, outcomes, rewards, and legal actions; neither sees hidden rules.

Falsifier: rival tie or loss, or paired confidence interval lower bound <=0.15. Long-B and noisy-feedback variants are additional diagnostics. No dev or held-out runs have been executed. A positive result is not evidence of a multi-timescale organ or functional consciousness.
